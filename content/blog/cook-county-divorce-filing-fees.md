Court fees and procedures can change. Before filing a divorce case in Cook County, verify the current amount and accepted payment methods directly with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Cook County.

## Verify the Filing Fee Before You Go

Use the clerk's current official fee schedule or contact the clerk. Ask which fee applies to your filing and whether your case requires any additional local forms or charges.

Do not rely on an old blog post, screenshot, or third-party fee table. Confirm the amount again close to the filing date.

## Costs That May Be Separate

Depending on the case, possible costs can include service, certified copies, document preparation outside Fresh Start IL's supported scope, mediation, evaluations, or professional assistance. Applicability and price vary; verify each item with the responsible official office or provider before paying.

## If You Cannot Afford the Filing Fee

Illinois Courts publishes fee-waiver forms and instructions. Eligibility and required proof depend on the current rules and your circumstances. Use the current official Illinois Courts materials and follow the circuit clerk's filing instructions. Only the court decides whether a waiver is granted.

## Before Filing

- Confirm venue and the correct filing location.
- Confirm the current filing fee and accepted payment methods.
- Check the circuit clerk's current local requirements.
- Review the current Illinois Courts fee-waiver materials if needed.
- Keep receipts and copies of everything submitted.
