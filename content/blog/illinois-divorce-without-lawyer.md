Illinois permits people to represent themselves, often called proceeding **pro se**. Self-representation does not change the law, filing rules, or evidence requirements, and it is not a fit for every situation.

## When to Consider Legal Help

Consider speaking with a lawyer if the case involves disputed parenting issues, abuse or coercion, hidden assets, a business, complex retirement benefits, immigration or tax concerns, or an inability to reach agreement. Only a lawyer can give legal advice about your rights and facts.

## Residence, Venue, and Grounds

Illinois law includes a 90-day residence condition tied to commencement of the action or entry of judgment, depending on timing. Venue is a separate question, and military-service facts can matter. Verify the current rules before filing.

Illinois uses irreconcilable differences as the ground for dissolution. Six months of living separate and apart creates a rebuttable presumption related to that ground; it is not described here as a universal pre-filing wait.

## A General Filing Sequence

1. **Identify unresolved issues.** Property, debt, maintenance, parenting responsibilities, parenting time, and support may need to be addressed.
2. **Find current forms and instructions.** Use current Illinois Courts materials and check the appropriate circuit clerk's local requirements.
3. **Prepare only the forms that apply.** Required documents vary by facts, case stage, and court. A Financial Affidavit may be required depending on the case, rules, or court order; it is not presented as universal here.
4. **Verify venue, filing method, and fees.** Confirm these directly with the appropriate clerk close to filing.
5. **Follow current service rules.** Do not assume a waiver or a particular response deadline applies; read the official papers and instructions.
6. **Follow court scheduling instructions.** Agreement does not guarantee a particular hearing procedure, timeline, or judgment.

## Avoid Common Assumptions

- Do not assume every county uses exactly the same document set.
- Do not assume leaving a field blank is acceptable; follow the form instructions.
- Do not use an old fee, deadline, or service number without verification.
- Do not assume software can determine whether an agreement protects your legal rights.
