Dear Will County Circuit Clerk's Office,

We prepare self-help document-preparation materials for self-represented litigants in uncontested dissolution matters and want to describe Will County procedure accurately. We are not a law firm and are not asking for legal advice—only procedural guidance about which published instruction applies to which document.

We found three official instructions that we cannot reconcile from the published sources alone:

1. Local Rule 8.09(C) — file after employer service

The Twelfth Judicial Circuit Local Court Rules, Version 13, revised October 1, 2023, state:

“An Income Withholding for Support may be served upon a Support payor’s employer upon the entry of an Order of Support or for an existing Order of Support. Any Income Withholding for Support served upon the employer shall be filed with the Circuit Clerk and notice shall be provided to all parties.”

2. The Clerk's proposed-order workflow — “Withholding Order”

The Circuit Clerk's “Instructions for Electronic Filing of Proposed Court Orders for Family Cases” state that proposed prove-up documents—including a “Support Order/Withholding Order”—should be submitted under the same eFile envelope, each as a separate PDF, and that proposed orders will not be entered unless first presented on the record in court.

3. The statewide instruction — do not file

The Illinois Courts instruction “How to Get an Order for Support,” ATJ 127.3 (09/25), says regarding the Income Withholding for Support:

“complete this form only after you have a signed Order for Support. Do not file this form with the Circuit Clerk.”

The companion statewide materials direct sending the form to the employer with the Order for Support and Letter to Employer, or requesting service through HFS.

Could you please clarify:

1. Do Rule 8.09(C)'s “Income Withholding for Support,” the Clerk's proposed “Withholding Order,” and the federal Income Withholding for Support form bearing OMB control number 0970-0154 refer to the same instrument or to different instruments?

2. If they are the same instrument, does Rule 8.09(C)'s direction to file after employer service apply in Will County notwithstanding ATJ 127.3's instruction not to file it? If so, which filing code or channel should a self-represented litigant use?

3. If they are different instruments, which document is the proposed “Withholding Order” in the prove-up workflow? Is the federal IWO ever filed, presented, or lodged with the Clerk or court in Will County, or is it only served on the employer?

4. What form of “notice … to all parties” does Rule 8.09(C) contemplate after employer service: notice of the fact of service, or service of the complete instrument? How should a self-represented litigant provide it?

5. Is there a current standing order, administrative order, or judge-specific instruction that supplements or supersedes Rule 8.09(C) or the published proposed-order instructions on this point?

We would be glad to review any published resource we may have missed. Thank you for your time and for the resources your offices publish for self-represented litigants.

Respectfully,

FreshStart IL Support Team
support@freshstart-il.com
https://freshstart-il.com
